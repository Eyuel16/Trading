The Curriculum

6 technical modules. Zero hype. 100% free.

Module 1: Blockchain Fundamentals

What is a distributed ledger? Hashing, blocks, and the immutability trilemma.

Module 2: Public/Private Keys

How wallets work. Seed phrases vs. passwords. The math behind ownership.

Module 3: Exchanges & Order Books
