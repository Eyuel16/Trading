The Crypto Trading Curriculum

6 technical modules. Zero fluff. Focused on execution and risk management.

Module 1: Market Mechanics

How crypto order books work. Bid/ask spread, market depth, and price discovery.

Module 2: Leverage & Margin

Cross vs. Isolated margin. Calculating liquidation prices and maintaining collateral.

Module 3: Order Types

Limit, Market, Stop-Loss, Take-Profit, and OCO (One-Cancels-Other) orders.

Module 4: Technical Analysis

Support/Resistance, RSI, MACD, Volume Profile, and VWAP for entry/exit timing.

Module 5: Risk Management

Position sizing (Kelly Criterion), risk/reward ratios, and handling drawdowns.

Module 6: The Trading Lab

Live execution walkthrough: on-ramp USDT, place a long/short trade, manage a position.
